# Code of Conduct

Devoal is for learners, many of them beginners and many of them young. Be kind and be useful.

- Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, insults, or discrimination.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent. Ask before assuming.
- Beginners' questions are welcome here. Never mock someone for not knowing something.
- No sexual content, no doxxing, no spam, no promotion unrelated to the project.
- Don't submit content you do not have the right to share, and do not copy lesson text from books or paid courses.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may ban repeat offenders.
Report problems by opening a private security advisory or emailing the maintainers listed in the repository profile.
